# Telogica Limited — Company Information

## Overview

**Telogica Limited** (formerly Aishwarya Technologies and Telecom Ltd) is a leading designer and manufacturer of defense-grade RF power amplifiers and RF components, serving critical defense, aerospace, telecom, and transportation sectors.

- **Established:** 1995
- **Listed:** BSE: 532975
- **Certifications:** ISO 9001:2015, SEBI LODR Compliant
- **Headquarters:** Hyderabad, Telangana, India
- **Team:** 75+ professionals from India's finest institutions

---

## Business Focus

### RF Power Amplifiers & Components (100 MHz–40 GHz)
- Radar systems (search, tracking, fire-control)
- Electronic Warfare (EW) systems
- Satellite Communications (SATCOM)
- Missile systems and guidance
- Unmanned Aerial Vehicles (UAVs)
- Defense-grade custom subsystems

### Test & Measurement Equipment
- **Optical:** Fusion splicers, OTDR systems, cable locators
- **RF:** Spectrum analyzers, network analyzers, signal generators
- **Telecom:** SDH analyzers, BTS testers, site analyzers
- **Infrastructure:** Network and infrastructure testing solutions
